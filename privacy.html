PRIVACY POLICY

Cindera

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Cyber Resilience Lab

Summary: Cindera does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you need to use it stays on your own device.

1. Who we are

Cindera (“the app”) is a self-hosted cybersecurity threat-intelligence brief developed by Nagaraj Vangapally under Cyber Resilience Lab (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, request, or transmit any personal data. Specifically, Cindera does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, or device identifiers
  • Usage analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers
  • Any health, patient, or clinical information

The app has no user accounts and no sign-up. There is no backend server that receives your information.

3. Information stored only on your device

  • Group passphrase (optional). If you choose “Remember on this device”, the shared access passphrase is stored only in your device’s local app storage so the brief can unlock automatically. It is never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • App settings. Your chosen brief URL and notification preference are stored locally on your device.

This information never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Biometric unlock

If enabled, Cindera uses your device’s built-in biometric check (fingerprint or face) to unlock the app. This is handled entirely by your device’s operating system. We never receive, see, or store your biometric data.

5. Notifications

Cindera may show local notifications when new critical or healthcare-sector security items appear in the brief. These notifications are generated on your device and contain only a generic message (for example, “New critical alert — open to view”). They never contain personal, patient, or sensitive information.

6. Network connections

To display the brief, the app loads your configured Cindera web page (hosted on static infrastructure such as GitHub Pages) and checks a small, non-personal update signal. These requests contain no personal data about you. As with any web request, the hosting provider may process standard technical data such as IP address to deliver the page; this is governed by that provider’s own policy and is not collected or retained by us.

7. Third-party content

The brief aggregates headlines and links from public cybersecurity sources and links back to them. When you open a link, you leave Cindera and visit that third-party site, which has its own privacy policy.

8. Children

Cindera is a professional security tool intended for adults and is not directed at children.

9. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we do not collect any.

10. Your choices

You can clear the locally stored passphrase and settings at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Cyber Resilience Lab (Nagaraj Vangapally) at the contact address listed on the app’s store page.
